Listen.

Three moves, and the page reads to you

  1. Select

    Highlight a paragraph, or right-click an empty spot and choose "Read full article".

  2. Press Alt+R

    Or use the right-click menu. Reading starts right away, in a voice that matches the text.

  3. Follow along

    The current word and sentence light up on the page, in the color you pick.

Local.

Your text stays on your computer

Simple TTS speaks through the voices your browser and system already have. It never sends what you read anywhere, and it has no servers to send it to.

Anywhere.

Three ways to keep the player

  • Floating

    A small player over the page. Move it, resize it, and it remembers where you left it.

  • Window

    The player in a window of its own, so you can switch tabs and stay in control.

  • Hidden

    No player on screen at all. Control it from the toolbar icon and the shortcuts.

Keys.

Keyboard shortcuts

Everything works from the keyboard. These are the defaults:

Alt+RRead the selected text
Alt+PPause and resume
Alt+SStop
Alt+.Speed up
Not set, add your ownSlow down
Not set, add your ownNext and previous paragraph

Change any of them, or give keys to the ones without, at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

Questions.

Frequently asked questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Every feature is free, with no subscription, no account and no ads.

Which voices can I use?

The ones your browser and operating system offer. How many there are and how natural they sound depends on your system. You can usually add more voices in your system’s speech or accessibility settings.

Does my text leave my computer?

Simple TTS never sends it anywhere. With your system’s local voices everything stays on the device and works offline. Some browsers also offer online voices; if you pick one of those, that voice service handles the text.

How do I read a whole article?

Right-click an empty part of the page, without selecting anything, and choose "Read full article". To start from a specific point, right-click a word and choose "Read from here to the end".

Can I change the shortcuts?

Yes. Open chrome://extensions/shortcuts in the address bar and set the keys you like.

Something is not working. Who do I tell?

Write to extensiones@abdell.dev saying which page you were on and what happened.
